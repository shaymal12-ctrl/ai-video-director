# Cinematography vocabulary (pick deliberately, one choice per category)

Every shot answers: what is the subject, what does the camera do, through what glass, from where,
in what light, in what colour, framed how. Models follow concrete film words better than
adjectives like "epic" or "beautiful".

## Camera bodies / formats (the "look")
- **ARRI Alexa 35 / Alexa Mini LF** - clean, filmic skin, modern features and ads.
- **RED V-Raptor** - crisp, high detail, commercials, food.
- **Sony Venice 2** - smooth highlights, night scenes.
- **35mm film (Kodak Vision3 500T / 250D)** - grain, halation, nostalgia, period pieces.
- **16mm film** - documentary texture, music videos. **Super 8** - memories, flashbacks.
- **iPhone / phone vertical** - UGC, POV, livestream authenticity.
- **GoPro / action cam** - POV action, fisheye distortion.

## Lenses
- **Focal length:** 14-24mm wide (space, distortion, energy) · 35mm (natural, storytelling) ·
  50mm (human eye) · 85mm (portrait, compressed background) · 100mm macro (food, texture,
  droplets) · 135-200mm tele (isolation, compression, voyeur).
- **Character:** anamorphic (oval bokeh, horizontal flares, 2.39 feel) · vintage primes
  (Cooke, Helios: soft, warm flare) · probe lens (bug's-eye macro travelling through food) ·
  tilt-shift (miniature) · fisheye.
- **Aperture:** f/1.4-2 shallow depth of field, creamy bokeh · f/8-11 everything sharp.

## Shot sizes
Extreme wide (ELS) · wide (WS) · full (FS) · medium (MS) · medium close-up (MCU) · close-up (CU) ·
extreme close-up (ECU) · insert (hands, object) · over-the-shoulder (OTS) · two-shot · POV.

## Angles
Eye level (neutral) · low angle (power, hero) · high angle (vulnerable) · bird's-eye / top-down
(food flat-lay, patterns) · worm's-eye · Dutch tilt (unease) · over-the-shoulder.

## Camera movement (one main move per shot, say speed and direction)
- **Push-in / dolly in** - tension, focus, emotion. **Pull-out / dolly out** - reveal, loneliness.
- **Truck / lateral slide** - follow along a counter, a table, a line of plates.
- **Orbit / arc** - hero product, a character's realization.
- **Crane / jib up or down** - reveal the place, open or close a story.
- **Tilt up/down, pan left/right** - reveal height, follow action.
- **Handheld** - urgency, documentary, horror. **Steadicam/gimbal follow** - immersive walk.
- **FPV drone dive** - energy, through windows/doors, over crowds.
- **Snorricam / bodycam** - disorientation. **Whip pan** - fast transition.
- **Rack focus** - move attention from foreground to background.
- **Dolly zoom (vertigo)** - shock. **Speed ramp / slow motion 120fps** - pours, drops, impacts.
- **Static locked-off** - comedy timing, tableau, contemplation.

## Lighting
- **Key styles:** low-key (dark, dramatic, food ads) · high-key (bright, clean, commercial) ·
  chiaroscuro (strong contrast) · Rembrandt (triangle on the cheek) · silhouette / backlight ·
  rim light (separates subject from black) · practicals (lamps, string lights, candles in frame).
- **Natural:** golden hour, blue hour, overcast soft, harsh noon, window light, moonlight.
- **Motivated colour:** neon signs, fire flicker, TV glow, police lights.
- **Atmosphere:** haze/volumetric light beams, steam, dust in backlight, rain, fog.
- **Food:** strong backlight to make steam and sugar glow, warm key from the side, black negative fill.

## Colour and grade
- Palettes: teal & orange · warm amber / cream / burgundy (bakery) · desaturated cold (thriller) ·
  pastel (comedy) · monochrome + one accent colour · Technicolor saturation · bleach bypass.
- Name the film stock or grade: "Kodak 2383 print look", "soft film grain", "lifted blacks".
- Keep one palette for the whole film; grade every shot to match (see edit-sound.md).

## Composition
Rule of thirds · centered symmetry (Wes Anderson) · leading lines · frame within a frame (doors,
windows) · negative space (room for text!) · foreground layers (leaves, glass) for depth ·
headroom/lead room · for 9:16: subject in the upper-middle, keep the bottom 20% clean for captions.
