# Ideas, trends, hooks and growth

## 1. Finding trends (live, never from memory)

Trends expire in days. Research them each time with web search (use the thorough/extended mode for
"what is trending now"), and cite the sources and dates you found:

- TikTok Creative Center (trending hashtags, songs, creators by region - set Israel when relevant).
- Instagram Reels trending audio, YouTube Shorts trending, Google Trends (region + "past 7 days").
- What the niche's top accounts posted in the last 2 weeks: formats that repeat = a trend.
- Seasonal and local calendar: holidays, events, launches, weather, news hooks (avoid tragedy).

Turn a trend into a format, not a copy: keep the mechanic (the structure, the reveal, the sound
cue), replace the content with the user's world.

## 2. Idea engines (use 2-3 to get 3 genuinely different concepts)

- **What if + familiar world:** "What if David vs Goliath was a sports broadcast?"
- **Collision:** two worlds that never meet (biblical × livestream, bakery × heist film).
- **POV / role swap:** the object or animal tells the story; the villain's view.
- **Escalation ladder:** each shot bigger than the last; the last one impossible.
- **The reveal:** start with a mystery image, explain at the end (rewatch value).
- **Series hook:** an ending that demands part 2 (micro-drama, cliffhanger, counter, countdown).
- **Process porn:** satisfying making-of (dough, pour, cut, assemble) - food and craft gold.
- **Local pride:** a real place, a real person, a real accent; audiences share what feels theirs.

For each concept write: logline · hook (first 2 s) · why it gets shared · ending/payoff ·
production risk (what AI struggles with) · credit estimate.

## 3. The hook (0-2 seconds)

The first frame must already be moving and readable without sound.

- **Visual hooks:** extreme close-up in motion (cheese pull, sauce pour, eyes snapping open),
  something impossible, a face looking into the lens, a strong colour contrast, a countdown/counter.
- **Text hooks (on screen, max 6 words):** a question ("מתכננים חופשה?"), a challenge
  ("רק 1% יבחינו בזה"), a stake ("יש לה 60 שניות"), a promise ("ככה עושים רוזלך").
- **Audio hooks:** a sound before the picture (a gasp, a ping, a door), a beat drop on the first cut.
- Do not open on a logo or a slow establishing shot. The logo can be in the corner from frame 1.

## 4. Retention through the middle

- A new visual idea every 1-2 s (cut, push-in, reveal, text beat). No dead seconds.
- Open loops: promise something early, pay it off late.
- Rhythm: cut on the music beat; vary shot length (short-short-long).
- Pattern break around 60-70% (silence, a reverse, a speed ramp, a POV switch).
- Faces and hands hold attention; empty rooms lose it.

## 5. Endings that grow followers

- Payoff + call to action that fits ("Part 2 tomorrow", "Which one would you order?").
- Loop endings (last frame matches the first) boost rewatches.
- Credit card: creator name + @handle for 1-1.5 s, after the story ends.

## 6. Platform specs

| Platform | Ratio | Ideal length | Safe zones (1080x1920) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reels | 9:16 | 7-30 s (up to 90) | keep text out of the bottom ~380 px and right ~140 px |
| TikTok | 9:16 | 15-35 s | bottom ~420 px (caption) and right ~150 px (buttons), top ~150 px |
| Shorts | 9:16 | 15-45 s | bottom ~350 px |
| YouTube | 16:9 | any | title-safe 90% |

Loudness about -14 LUFS, true peak ≤ -1 dBTP. Hook text centered in the upper-middle third.
